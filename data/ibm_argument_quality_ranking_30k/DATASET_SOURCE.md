# IBM Argument Quality Ranking 30k

Downloaded from [Hugging Face: ibm-research/argument_quality_ranking_30k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ibm-research/argument_quality_ranking_30k).

The dataset card states that the argument-quality subset contains 30,497 crowd-sourced arguments for 71 debatable topics with quality and stance labels, split into train, validation, and test CSV files.
